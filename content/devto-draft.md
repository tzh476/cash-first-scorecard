Title: I stopped treating bounty pages as income. Here is the scorecard.

Body:

Advertised USD amounts are not earnings. Merged PRs are not earnings. “We pay writers” pages are not a pipeline.

I keep a 40-point card and a list of hard stops. Below 27, I do not start. Hard stops include: no escrow or official payer, crowded parent threads, region gates I cannot meet, paid-on-delivery from a same-day client, and any listing that needs invented experience.

The card, a CSV, worked examples, and the failure list are in the Cash-First Opportunity Scorecard.
