Advertised USD amounts are not earnings. A merged PR is not earnings. A “we pay writers” page is not a pipeline.

I learned this the slow way: opening listings that were already rewarded, bidding into unfunded gigs, and treating prize pools as if they were invoices. The fix was not “work harder.” It was a 15-minute scorecard and a short list of hard stops.

## The rule

Score the opportunity before you write code, a pitch, or a proposal. Maximum 40 points. **Below 27, do not start.** Any hard stop is an immediate no.

Hard stops I actually use:

- Reward already paid, reserved, or closed
- No escrow, official program, or documented payer
- Parent thread is a claim farm
- Region or ID gate you cannot truthfully meet
- Wallet lock, unpaid video, or hardware you do not have
- You would have to invent experience
- New client, paid on delivery, no funded milestone

If it passes, score payment trust, whether the task is still live, competition, acceptance clarity, skill fit, local verification, maintainer response, and time. Then compute expected value with your hourly floor. Advertised prize pools are not the numerator.

## What “paid” means

I only move a row to `paid` when the payer confirms the money moved. Merge, “we’ll review,” and a registration receipt stay at `submitted` or `candidate`.

Public traces of that distinction:

- Official event bounties that paid only after PayPal confirmation
- A maintainer-paid bugfix that was `merged_pending_payment` until the receipt arrived

The method is in the kit. The kit is not a list of live leads.
