Subject: Pitch: Dashboards that are allowed to say “I don’t know”

Hi,

I’d like to propose a 1,500-word essay: “Dashboards that are allowed to say I don’t know.”

Thesis: most status pages treat a green check as a conclusion. For operators, a more honest interface is one that can render unknown, stale, or unverified as first-class states — and that this is a design problem, not a monitoring-vendor feature.

Outline:
1. The lie of a single green CI or uptime badge
2. Three freshness states: verified, stale, unknown
3. A small public example (https://github.com/tzh476/evidence-first-static-dashboard)
4. What this changes for incident copy and on-call trust
5. Limits: this is not a full observability platform
