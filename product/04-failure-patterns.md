# Failure patterns

Exit as soon as you recognize one of these. Do not “just peek at the code.”

1. **Stale bounty.** Aggregator still shows money; the issue is closed or labeled rewarded.
2. **Reward removed.** Maintainer switched to sponsorships or said the bounty is gone.
3. **Crowded parent thread.** Mass `/claim`, scoped child PRs, or a long reward table. A clean child issue does not rescue a noisy parent.
4. **Wrong contribution type.** Repo or editor rejects first-time or AI-assisted work.
5. **Research sandbox.** README says humans need not apply, or there is no real payout.
6. **Unverifiable acceptance.** Needs a vendor account, GPU, or device you do not have.
7. **Huge prize, silent repo.** High number, months without review, no escrow.
8. **Hidden submission cost.** Video, paper, or travel eats the advertised pay.
9. **Legal action you cannot proxy.** Terms, CLA, tax form, or KYC must be accepted by the person who gets paid.
10. **Marketplace theater.** New buyer, paid-on-delivery, or “send files first.”
11. **Region fiction.** Listing is silent on country, then the form rejects your real location. Do not use a VPN to invent eligibility.
12. **Unpriced intake.** “We pay writers” with no rate, no assignment, and a CV wall. Registering is not a pipeline.

When in doubt, label it `excluded` and move the time budget to a new first-party source.
